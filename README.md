# neon-snake

Experience the ultimate retro vibe with Neon Snake! Navigate a glowing grid, collect neon pellets to grow your tail, and avoid crashing into walls or yourself. Featuring responsive swipe and keyboard controls, vibrant arcade aesthetics, and thrilling speed progression. Can you survive and set a new high score?

## 🎮 Features
*   **Retro Arcade Aesthetics:** Glowing neon graphics, grid-based movement, and dynamic shadows.
*   **Responsive Controls:** 
    *   **Desktop:** Use `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys` to navigate.
    *   **Mobile:** Intuitive swipe controls (swipe up, down, left, right to steer).
*   **Dynamic Difficulty:** The snake's speed increases gradually as you eat more food.
*   **High Score Tracking:** Your highest score is automatically saved in your browser's local storage.
*   **Plug-and-Play:** The entire game is contained within a single HTML file—no downloads, installations, or servers required.

## 🕹️ How to Play
*   **Objective:** Eat the glowing pink food nodes to increase your score and grow your snake. 
*   **Rule 1:** Do not hit the edges of the screen.
*   **Rule 2:** Do not run into your own tail.
*   **Tip:** The game gets slightly faster every time you eat a piece of food, so plan your path carefully!

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
*   **HTML5 Canvas:** For rendering the grid, snake, food, and high-performance glow effects.
*   **Vanilla JavaScript:** Core game logic, input handling, and the animation loop.
*   **Tailwind CSS:** (Loaded via CDN) For quick, responsive UI overlays and typography.
